# Surface brief: /concept-a (homepage, Concept A)

Scope: homepage prototype for Concept A, refined to "Modern Scandinavian Premium Commerce" (user brief, 2026-11-17). Visitor mode: Persuade, with a strong shopping-task layer. Build path: code-led.
Audience and job: club secretaries, show and event organisers, and breeders who need the right award before an event date. The two primary journeys are Shop products and Design your own rosette.
Proof and content: only confirmed live-site and reference content. Unconfirmed facts render as "To confirm". No reviews or testimonials. OPT-1 to OPT-7 are off. Client images from intersilvi.com are used with permission; Unsplash stand-ins are marked "Provisional".
Constraints: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v3 only, tokens in src/concepts/concept-a/tokens.ts (namespace a-), server components by default, typed mock data.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Intersilvi's own navy and yellow, used at full strength, set against real event and product photography, with Scandinavian discipline in type and layout. It refuses grey-on-grey e-commerce, rows of identical cards and generic luxury styling.
OWN-WORLD: White and one cool linen as grounds. Navy-deep blocks for the hero panel, the story and the footer. One full-yellow section owns "design your own". Yellow always means the design journey and navy always means shop. Schibsted Grotesk bold display up to 6rem at -0.04em tracking. 2px corners and hairlines. Real product cutouts stand on stone plinths. Event photos are set in a mosaic with solid white label plates and no gradient overlays.
STORY: Within one screen the visitor sees a horse wearing a rosette, reads "Award rosettes, trophies and medals", and picks yellow (design) or outline (shop). Scrolling, they see delivery times, discovery by event, the five design steps on yellow, real products with quantity prices, ribbon materials, craft and story, then help.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A navy utility bar, then a white header with the logo, search, account and cart, and the navigation row. Below, a full-bleed photo with the horse and rosette on the left, and a solid navy panel flush to the right edge holding a three-line bold H1, a yellow second line, the intro, and the yellow "Design your own rosette" button beside the outline "Explore products" button. On mobile the photo sits on top and both buttons appear before the intro.
FORM: seed: none (pinned). The user pinned it in these words: "Refine Concept A as Modern Scandinavian Premium Commerce … a compelling premium hero using authentic, brand-relevant award or equestrian photography … two clearly differentiated actions: Design your own rosette, Explore products". The signature moment is the event mosaic, with its asymmetric photo tiles, and the hanging-ribbon colour rack.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
